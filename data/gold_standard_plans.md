## How to Use These Benchmarks

### For Stanley (Technical)
1. Feed each persona profile into the AI system
2. Compare AI-generated plan against these benchmarks
3. Score using the rubric in `persona_profiles.yaml`
4. Target: AI plan scores ≥ 10/14 for each persona

### For Blessie (Clinical)
1. Review AI-generated plans independently
2. Score against these gold standards
3. Note where AI exceeds or falls short of benchmarks
4. Provide qualitative feedback on plan quality

### For the Paper
These benchmarks form the "Gold Standard" comparison group:
- AI Plan Score / Gold Standard Score = Relative Quality Index
- RQI ≥ 0.85 is considered "non-inferior"
- RQI ≥ 1.0 is considered "equivalent or superior"
