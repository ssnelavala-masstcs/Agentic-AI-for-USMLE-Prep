# Using Your Study Plan

Tips for getting the most out of your AI-generated USMLE study plan.

## Daily Study Routine

### Before Each Block

1. **Review the block details**: Note the topic, duration, and question target
2. **Gather resources**: Have UWorld, First Aid, and other materials ready
3. **Set a timer**: Stick to the allocated hours

### During the Block

1. **Start with practice questions** (active recall first!)
2. **Review explanations** for both correct and incorrect answers
3. **Take notes** on concepts you missed
4. **Use the Pomodoro technique**: 50 min study, 10 min break

### After Each Block

1. **Log your performance**: Record questions attempted/correct
2. **Note confidence level**: How well did you feel? (1-5 scale)
3. **Mark completed**: Update the system so it can adapt your plan

## Review Day Strategy

Every 3rd day is a comprehensive review day. Here's how to maximize it:

### Morning Session (Weak Area Intensive)

- Focus on your **lowest proficiency area**
- Review all incorrect UWorld questions from the past week
- Re-read First Aid sections on missed topics
- Target: 50-60 practice questions

### Afternoon Session (Mixed Practice)

- Do 40-60 mixed questions (simulates exam conditions)
- Don't look up answers until you've completed the block
- Review all explanations thoroughly
- Track your accuracy by topic

## Adapting to Your Plan

### When a Topic is Too Hard

If you're scoring < 40% on practice questions:

1. **Don't panic**—this is exactly why the system identified it as a weak area!
2. **Spend extra time** on foundational concepts
3. **Use additional resources** (Pathoma videos, Boards & Beyond)
4. **The system will notice** and adjust your next plan accordingly

### When a Topic is Too Easy

If you're scoring > 85% consistently:

1. **Move faster** through the material
2. **Focus on details** you might be missing
3. **The system will reduce** time allocation in future plans

### Missing a Study Day

Life happens. If you miss a day:

1. **Don't try to "catch up"** by doubling the next day
2. **Resume with the current day's plan**
3. **The system will reschedule** missed content in future plans
4. **Log what you actually studied** (even if it wasn't the plan)

## Maximizing Score Improvement

### High-Yield Strategies

1. **Prioritize UWorld**: It's the single best resource. Do every question at least once.
2. **Review incorrects thoroughly**: Understanding why you missed a question is more valuable than getting it right.
3. **Use First Aid as a reference**: Don't read it cover-to-cover; use it to look up concepts you miss.
4. **Take NBME practice exams**: Every 2 weeks to track progress.

### IMG-Specific Tips

1. **US Healthcare System**: Dedicate extra time to ethics, preventive medicine, and biostatistics
2. **Clinical Vignettes**: Practice reading long question stems quickly
3. **Drug Names**: Focus on US brand names (not just generic/international names)
4. **Lab Values**: Memorize US reference ranges (may differ from your country)

## Common Questions

### Q: How many hours should I study per day?

**A**: 6-8 hours is typical for dedicated study. The system adapts to your available time. Quality > quantity—focused, active study for 6 hours beats distracted studying for 10.

### Q: Should I do UWorld timed or tutor mode?

**A**: 
- **Weeks 1-2**: Tutor mode (learn at your pace)
- **Weeks 3+**: Timed (simulates exam pressure)
- **Review days**: Always timed

### Q: What if I disagree with the plan's prioritization?

**A**: The plan is a recommendation. If you know a specific topic needs more/less time, adjust accordingly. **Log what you actually studied** so the system learns your preferences.

### Q: How often should I take NBME practice exams?

**A**: Every 2 weeks. More frequently doesn't give you time to improve; less frequently loses tracking resolution.

### Q: Can I use this for Step 2 CK?

**A**: Currently, the system is optimized for Step 1. Step 2 CK support is planned for future versions.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Plan feels too overwhelming

**Solution**: Reduce daily hours in your profile. The system will create shorter, more focused blocks.

### Problem: I'm not improving in my weak area

**Solution**: 
1. Check if you're actually doing the practice questions
2. Try different resources (maybe Pathoma works better than First Aid for you)
3. The system should automatically increase time allocation—verify this in your next plan

### Problem: The plan doesn't account for my clinical rotations

**Solution**: Update your available study hours. If you only have 4 hours/day during rotations, tell the system.
